Research

Our research focuses on advancing molecular imaging techniques, particularly in the context of nuclear medicine.

Total-Body PET Imaging

We develop methods that exploit the high sensitivity and extended axial field of view of total-body PET systems.

Our work focuses on improving image quality, reducing radiation dose, and enabling new quantitative imaging applications.

Artificial Intelligence

We develop deep-learning methods for synthetic-CT image generation, attenuation correction, denoising, and reconstruction.

Our goal is to create robust and clinically useful AI methods for molecular imaging.

Multimodal Imaging

We integrate PET with complementary imaging modalities such as CT and MRI to improve quantitative molecular imaging.

Our research explores how multimodal imaging can provide complementary anatomical and functional information and enable new applications beyond conventional PET imaging.

Quantitative Molecular Imaging

We develop techniques for accurate measurement of physiological and molecular processes using PET.

Applications include oncology, cardiovascular imaging, low-dose imaging, and kinetic analysis.
