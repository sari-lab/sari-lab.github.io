Recognition at Total-Body PET 2026

Our PhD student, Kathiresan Chandrasekaran received a USD 1,000 travel award and was selected as a finalist for the Early Career Research Poster Award at the Total-Body PET Conference 2026 at UC Davis for his work on CT dose reduction in cardiac LAFOV PET imaging.

April 2026

New SNSF Grant for PET Perfusion-Metabolism Imaging

Hasan Sari and Federico Caobelli received CHF 407,751 in project funding from the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) for the project “PET Perfusion-Metablism Imaging for Improved Characterization of Suspected Lung Cancer Lesions.” The project will investigate PET perfusion and metabolism imaging for improved characterization of suspected lung cancer lesions and will run from 2026 to 2029.
